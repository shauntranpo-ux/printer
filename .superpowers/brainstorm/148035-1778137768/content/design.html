Part B — BV3 Recalibration

Method: For each settled market, simulate a 3-min-before-close entry, compute distance_bucket + time_bucket, record outcome (result=1 → YES won)
Output: New BV3 table written to bot_strategy.py — replaces the April-calibrated table
